KASA umbrella twins, fixed identity. Put this block at the top of every image prompt for these characters and attach the identity reference image (mobile/assets/original/twins.png, or sky.png / amber.png for a single sister).

Two original chibi sisters, about 2.5 heads tall, crisp dark navy outlines, flat light cel shading, soft pastel palette, clean hand-drawn chibi finish.
The surface is completely matte, like a printed illustration: flat fills with at most one soft shadow tone per color. No glossy highlights, specular shine or white shine streaks on the hair, umbrella or clothes, and no plastic, vinyl, glassy, 3D or airbrushed look.

Sky, the older sister (always on the LEFT when they are together): very long pale sky-blue twin tails reaching below the knees, two simple blue ribbon bows, soft bangs, blue eyes, calm gentle expression (half-lidded eyes or a small closed-mouth smile), light pink blush lines. Quiet and composed.

Amber, the younger sister (always on the RIGHT): pale amber-blonde hair in two round buns with short tails hanging below them, small amber-yellow ribbon bows on the buns, amber-gold eyes, open-mouth cheerful smile. Energetic; usually waves or makes a V sign.

Each sister owns one color. Sky is sky blue, Amber is amber yellow; hair, eyes, bows and accent clothing follow that color. Never swap or mix the colors, and no heterochromia.

They share ONE umbrella split exactly in half: the sky-blue half over Sky, the amber half over Amber, a thin dark pole and a curved dark J handle held by Sky.

Outfit A, rainwear (default): Sky wears a short sky-blue hooded rain cape with one button and drawstrings over an ivory dress; Amber wears an amber hooded rain jacket with buttons and pockets over an ivory dress; both wear dark navy rain boots with cream socks.

Outfit B, everyday: matching white short-sleeve blouses with a soft collar and a ribbon tie in each sister's own color (blue for Sky, amber for Amber), white flared skirts, white socks, black strap shoes.

Never add halos, school insignia, badges, crests, weapons, game or franchise symbols, extra characters, text, lettering, logos or watermarks.
